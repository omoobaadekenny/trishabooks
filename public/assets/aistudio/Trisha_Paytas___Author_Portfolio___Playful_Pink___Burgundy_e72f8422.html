Beyond the headlines

“A life lived in public. A story still unfolding.”

Trisha Paytas is a media personality and one of the internet’s early influencers. She posted her first YouTube video in 2007 and has since built a following of more than 20 million fans across platforms.

Across her career, she has appeared in more than 50 television shows and films, including Saturday Night Live, Celebrity Big Brother UK, Euphoria, Hacks, and America’s Got Talent, according to her publisher.

Her memoir turns the lens inward, reflecting on public reinvention, the pursuit of fame, and the search for a more grounded sense of self.
